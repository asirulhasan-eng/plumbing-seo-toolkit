# Google Business Profile Checklist for Plumbers

Use this checklist to find common Google Business Profile issues that can cost a plumbing company calls from Google Maps.

Source: [PlumbingSEO.agency](https://plumbingseo.agency/) - local SEO for plumbers.

## Core Profile Setup

- Business name matches the real-world company name.
- Primary category is set to Plumber when accurate.
- Secondary categories match real services, not random keywords.
- Phone number is correct and answered during business hours.
- Website URL points to the best local plumbing landing page.
- Appointment or contact URL points to a fast lead form or booking path.
- Business hours are current.
- Emergency or 24-hour hours are clearly reflected if offered.
- Service areas match where the trucks actually go.

## Service Details

- Main plumbing services are listed.
- Emergency plumbing is listed if offered.
- Drain cleaning is listed if offered.
- Water heater repair and installation are listed if offered.
- Sewer line services are listed if offered.
- Leak detection is listed if offered.
- Commercial plumbing is listed if offered.

## Trust Signals

- Recent customer photos are added.
- Branded truck photos are added.
- Team or owner photos are added.
- Reviews mention specific services and cities naturally.
- Review responses are helpful, short, and local when appropriate.
- Profile posts are used for service updates, seasonal needs, and audit-worthy offers.

## Local SEO Leaks to Watch

- Service area is too broad or inaccurate.
- Website has no matching service-area pages.
- Photos look generic or stock-like.
- Reviews are stale.
- Business description is stuffed with keywords.
- Tracking number breaks NAP consistency.
- Profile links to a weak homepage instead of a call-focused page.

## Quick Fix Priority

1. Correct category, phone, URL, and service areas.
2. Add service details for the jobs you want more of.
3. Upload real photos of trucks, team, and work.
4. Build matching website pages for important services and cities.
5. Track calls from Google Maps.
