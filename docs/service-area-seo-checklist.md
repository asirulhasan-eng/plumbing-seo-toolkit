# Service Area SEO Checklist for Plumbers

Service-area SEO helps Google understand where a plumbing company works and which local searches should show that company.

Created by [PlumbingSEO.agency](https://plumbingseo.agency/).

## Page Planning

- List every city, town, and neighborhood the plumbing company actually serves.
- Prioritize areas with the best job value, response time, and existing customer base.
- Create unique pages for major service areas.
- Avoid copy-pasting the same city page with only the city name changed.
- Link service-area pages from the main navigation, footer, or service area hub.

## What Each Page Should Include

- Clear H1 using the city or service area.
- Short intro explaining plumbing services in that area.
- List of services available there.
- Local proof such as nearby neighborhoods, landmarks, reviews, or job examples.
- Internal links to service pages.
- Fast call CTA.
- Business NAP details where appropriate.
- FAQ section based on real customer questions.

## Local Signals

- Google Business Profile service areas align with website pages.
- Reviews mention the service area naturally.
- Site footer or contact page mentions core service areas.
- Internal links use natural anchors like "plumber in [city]".
- Schema markup supports local business details.

## Common Leaks

- Serving many cities but only having one homepage.
- Creating thin doorway pages with no local usefulness.
- Targeting cities outside the real service area.
- Missing calls to action.
- No link path from the homepage to city pages.
- No matching service pages for important jobs.
