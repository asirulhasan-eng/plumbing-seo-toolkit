# Plumbing SEO Leak Check Guide

A plumbing SEO leak check is a practical audit that finds where a plumbing company may be losing calls from Google.

Created by [PlumbingSEO.agency](https://plumbingseo.agency/).

## What to Inspect

Google Business Profile:
Check categories, service areas, photos, reviews, services, hours, and website link.

Google Maps visibility:
Check whether the business appears for high-intent local searches in its real service area.

Website structure:
Check whether the site has clear service pages, city pages, contact paths, and internal links.

Service pages:
Check whether important jobs like emergency plumbing, drain cleaning, water heaters, and sewer repair have dedicated pages.

Technical SEO:
Check speed, crawlability, indexing, title tags, meta descriptions, headings, schema, and broken links.

Call conversion:
Check whether the phone number is visible, tappable, and tracked correctly.

## Simple Scorecard

- 0-3 leaks: strong foundation
- 4-7 leaks: several missed-call risks
- 8+ leaks: likely losing calls to competitors

## Best Next Step

Fix the issues closest to revenue first: Maps visibility, phone CTA, service pages, and service-area pages.
